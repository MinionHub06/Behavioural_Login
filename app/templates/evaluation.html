Research Objectives & Findings

Review 0 Deliverables
Multi-Modal Feature Fusion: Combining aggregate keystroke dynamics (dwell/flight variance) with mouse movement kinetics (velocity, acceleration, curvature, jitter) and contextual environmental signals dramatically outperforms static credentials and legacy CAPTCHAs.
Adversarial Slow Mimicry Resistance: Capping the maximum allowable drift to 10% per update cycle ensures the rolling baseline adapts to legitimate behavioral aging while strictly repelling incremental poisoning attacks.
Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution: Every elevated risk score is decomposed into explicit, deterministic feature contributions, eliminating black-box opacity and equipping security teams with actionable audit logs.
